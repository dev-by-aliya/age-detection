Biological vs Apparent Age

Why your face age differs from your real age

Chronological age is purely time-based. Apparent facial age reflects UV exposure, hydration, stress levels, genetics, and sleep patterns. ageguesser analyzes facial structure, skin tone uniformity, and visible texture to estimate how old you look.
